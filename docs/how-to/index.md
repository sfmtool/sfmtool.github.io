# How-to guides

Each guide gets one job done, for a reader who has been through
[Getting Started](../tutorials/getting-started.md).

- [Inspecting a Reconstruction](../tutorials/inspecting.md): understand a
  reconstruction's properties, such as how its points and errors are
  distributed and how the camera moves, from the command line.
- [Converting Video to Image Sequences](../tutorials/video-to-images.md): turn
  a video into numbered images that `sfm` can solve.
